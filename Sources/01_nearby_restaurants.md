# Resident event catering catalog near UT Austin

Six real restaurants with group-size order examples. Locations and menu types were checked against official restaurant or UT pages on September 26, 2026. **Prices marked “practice estimate” are fictional classroom figures, not vendor quotes.** Cabo Bob's totals are calculated from the prices displayed on its catering pages on that date; confirm them when ordering. All totals exclude tax, tips, delivery, service fees, and any extras not listed. Check group-order availability with each location and get the required UT spending approval before buying anything.

The RA supplies the resident request, headcount, budget, timing, and supplies at run time. These tables are a catalog, not a preselected event plan.

## Agent use rules

- Match the resident's request, food restrictions, budget, timing and group size to relevant bulk packages. Compare serving quantities, not just subtotal prices; do not assume 10 pizzas feeds a fixed group size without checking slices and appetite.
- Show a **practice subtotal** for hypothetical costs, or a **posted-price calculation** for Cabo Bob's, then separately flag tax, delivery, tips and any unknown costs. Never represent sample prices or calculated totals as current invoices.
- Combine a food order with a destination only if that destination allows outside food. In particular, the Union Underground bars outside food/drinks other than cake or dessert, and museum galleries have food rules.
- Ask the RA to make the final vendor and event choices. Do not purchase, contact vendors or edit the sources.
