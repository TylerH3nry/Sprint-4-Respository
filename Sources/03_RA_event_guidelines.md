# RA event planning rules for this class project

This is a **hypothetical planning brief**, not an official UT Austin RA handbook. The $400 limit and outing boundary below are rules for this agent exercise supplied or defined for the project. For a real resident event, use your supervisor's current requirements and approved funding instead. The agent reads this file together with `01_nearby_restaurants.md` and `02_campus_outing_places.md`.

## Event budget: $400 maximum

- The **whole event** must cost no more than **$400**. Add food, drinks, dessert, place admissions or activity fees, group reservations, tax, delivery, service charges, tips, supplies, and travel costs if any. Do not treat $400 as the food budget alone.
- Use the food catalog's clearly marked practice prices only for **practice estimates**. Cabo Bob's posted-price calculations and venue admission rates still require date-specific confirmation. If taxes/fees are unknown, show a subtotal plus “final total unconfirmed”; do not promise the plan fits $400.
- Prefer a plan that leaves room for unknown costs. When costs exceed $400, show the gap and suggest a smaller group package or a free activity; do not silently increase the budget.
- Buying, booking, reimbursement, vendor approval and actual spending belong to the RA and Residence Life staff. This agent only drafts options.

## Outing boundary for this exercise

Residents may be taken to destinations **inside or directly on** these four boundary streets:

| Edge | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| North | West/East 38th Street |
| South | West/East 18th Street |
| West | North Lamar Boulevard |
| East | Interstate 35 |

**Use the venue's destination address for the outing check; do not apply this limit to a restaurant delivering food to an allowed destination.** The 21 entries in `02_campus_outing_places.md` were selected as campus or nearby candidates, but the agent must still check any new address against the boundary. A destination outside this rectangle is **out of scope** for this exercise; name it and ask the RA for an inside-the-boundary alternative. The route to a destination should also be reasonable and accessible for the group. This rectangle is deliberately broader than UT's main campus and is **not a university rule**.

It is not to scale; verify a proposed street address in [UT's official interactive campus map](https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/81d900a3c906482e9731a7a71eaaa178) or another current map. The rules above, not the drawing, determine eligibility. The image is outside `Sources/` so the Claude agent still uses only three text files.

## Match the request before suggesting anything

1. Read the RA's message for what residents want, expected attendance, date/time, budget if lower than $400, dietary needs, mobility/access needs, and supplies already on hand. If key details are missing, flag them; do not make up an approved headcount or venue availability.
2. Use either the **food catalog**, the **outing catalog**, or both depending on the request. Do not add an outing when residents asked only for food, or add food when they asked only for an activity.
3. For each suggestion, show the relevant catalog item, serving or group-size assumption, cost math, remaining budget, and details still to verify. Give no more than three options and then stop for the RA's choice.

## Venue and food compatibility

- **Texas Union Underground:** the venue says outside food and drinks are not allowed, except cake or dessert; other catering should use Forty Acres Catering. A restaurant pizza order is therefore not a bowling-alley food option. Source: https://universityunions.utexas.edu/food-fun/union-underground-reservations/
- **Texas Union reserved rooms:** University Unions describes Forty Acres Catering as the exclusive caterer for confirmed events in the Texas Union. Do not assume the restaurants in the food catalog can cater a reserved Union room. Source: https://universityunions.utexas.edu/reserve/food-catering
- **WCP Student Activity Center:** external catering may require an Environmental Health & Safety food distribution form and an approved permit; prepackaged food/drinks can require a WCP food waiver. Check the actual event's process with staff. Source: https://universityunions.utexas.edu/reserve/food-catering
- **Texas Science & Natural History Museum:** food and drinks other than water are not allowed inside; use allowed outdoor areas for snacks. Source: https://sciencemuseum.utexas.edu/plan-your-visit/
- **Other locations:** check venue-specific food, access, group-booking, and opening rules before combining them with an order. “Public admission” does not mean permission to host a private event.

## Human handoff and UT context

- Ask the RA to decide the final event, vendor, destination, and any creative choices about the experience. The agent must not contact, pay, reserve, or edit source files.
- UT Housing says student activity fund expenditures are initiated through Residence Hall Councils and approved by Residence Life staff. This public statement does **not** mean this exercise's $400 is an approved UT allocation. Source: https://housing.utexas.edu/housing/residence-halls/get-involved/leading-halls
- If resident information includes names or sensitive needs, summarize the relevant planning constraint without repeating personal details in the event proposal.
