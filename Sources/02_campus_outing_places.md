# UT Austin and nearby outing catalog for resident events

Locations, activities, and listed admission rates are from venue or UT pages checked September 26, 2026. “No ticket” means access to a publicly open area, **not permission to reserve it for a private program**. “Included” means access for eligible enrolled UT students with valid ID; an RA group reservation may have separate requirements. All dates, hours, group capacity, access needs, and funding approval must be checked for the actual event.

## How the agent should use the catalog

- Start with the resident's request. Match the activity type, location, expected attendance, date and budget; give only relevant places, not all 21.
- Add venue costs to any food proposal **only when food is allowed** and the combined estimates are meaningful. For example, do not bring Domino's pizza into the Underground or food into museum galleries.
- Say **price to confirm** when admission is show-specific or a group reservation quote is missing. Never use a department room rate as a confirmed RA rate. Flag missing venue capacity, travel arrangements, accessibility needs and approvals.
- The RA chooses the final plan and handles all reservations, payments, and outreach.
